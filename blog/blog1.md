Hi, I'm Luxor Bourommavong — a Data Science student at UNC Charlotte, expected to graduate in May 2027. This blog is where I'll be documenting what I'm learning as I go, starting with a bit about how I got here and where I'm headed.

My path into data science comes down to one belief: numbers only matter if they help people. I've always been drawn to work where data isn't just an abstraction on a screen, but a way of understanding real circumstances and real decisions — which is what pulled me specifically toward humanitarian and social-impact data work. Courses like Data Science for Social Good, Data Modeling, and Predictive Analytics have given me a technical foundation, but it's been hands-on projects — like cleaning and analyzing WHO vaccination coverage data, or using ArcGIS to map the lasting impact of unexploded ordinance across Southeast Asia — that showed me what that foundation is actually for.

Right now, I work as a Data Analyst/Writer for The Lookout Post, where I dig into datasets from organizations like the UN and WHO, clean and transform them using SQL and Excel, and build Python and Power BI dashboards to communicate findings to non-technical audiences. That last part matters to me as much as the analysis itself — a dataset nobody can act on doesn't accomplish much. I care about being able to explain what I find as much as finding it.

Looking ahead, my goal is to build a career doing exactly this kind of work at an NGO, UN agency, or similar organization — using Python, SQL, R, pandas, and Power BI to turn messy, real-world data into decisions that actually improve people's lives. This portfolio will track that journey: the coursework, the projects, and eventually the work itself. Thanks for stopping by.
